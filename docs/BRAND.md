# Name — Weft

**Weft** /wɛft/ — in weaving, the thread drawn *across* the warp to bind a fabric together.
Here: your live web sessions woven into your AI.

### Why it works
- **Cool & minimal** — one syllable, premium, lowercase-friendly (`weft`), pairs with a clean mark.
- **SEO-friendly** — near-zero competition in the dev-tool space, so the brand term ranks
  immediately and you own the SERP. Content targeting the *intent* ("convert website to MCP",
  "site to MCP server") is handled with docs/blog regardless of brand.
- **On-theme** — weaving = binding separate threads (sites) into one fabric (your AI's context).
- **Easy** — trivial to spell, say, and type; short package/namespace (`weft`), clean domains
  (weft.dev / weft.app / useweft.com).

### Verbs & surface copy
- "**Weave** this site into your AI" (extension button could read *Weave this site* or keep the
  literal *Convert to MCP* for clarity).
- MCPs are "**threads**" in marketing copy (optional); stay literal ("MCP"/"server") in the app UI.

### Alternatives (if Weft doesn't land)
- **Sidewire** — descriptive compound; evokes a side-channel wire from browser → AI (and the
  sidecar architecture). Unique, good SEO. Less premium.
- **Conduit** — safe real word, clear metaphor. Higher SEO competition; disambiguate as
  "Conduit MCP".

### Before committing
Check domain availability and do a trademark search (USPTO/EUIPO) — there have been minor,
mostly-defunct uses of "Weft" (old qualitative-analysis software). Low risk, but verify.

> Renaming later is one search-replace across the repo: the namespace `weft`, the app title,
> the `tauri.conf.json` identifier, and the WS/port constants.
